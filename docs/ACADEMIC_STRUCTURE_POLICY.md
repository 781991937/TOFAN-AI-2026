# Academic structure policy

## University references

Universities such as Sana'a University are stored as **reference structures only**.
Their names, colleges, centers, and specialties help TOFAN present a familiar
academic hierarchy. Their official course plans, lectures, assessments, and
study materials are **not copied into TOFAN**.

## TOFAN global curricula

TOFAN owns the learning curriculum. Each active TOFAN specialty must have a
registered curriculum in `docs/curricula/specialty_structure_v1.json`.

The canonical learning hierarchy is:

`Specialty -> Year -> Semester -> Course -> Knowledge Unit -> Topic -> Lesson -> Application -> Assessment -> Learning Outcome -> Project`

The default academic presentation is 4 years and 8 semesters, but progression
also respects prerequisites and demonstrated learning outcomes.

## Under-construction specialties

A specialty may be shown as an academic option while its curriculum is being
prepared, but it must remain inactive/unpublished until a registered TOFAN
curriculum exists. This prevents students from entering an empty or misleading
learning path.

## Source rule

Global references such as ACM/IEEE, NIST, ISO/IEC/IEEE, and other professional
or academic frameworks guide knowledge selection and learning outcomes. TOFAN
does not present those references as a copied university curriculum.
